# Privacy Policy for ProductivityPet

**Last updated: September 10, 2026**

ProductivityPet is a productivity application designed to help users manage tasks and interact with a virtual pet.

## Information We Collect

ProductivityPet does not require users to create an account or provide personal information.

Task information, progress, pet progress, and other app data are stored locally on the user's device. ProductivityPet does not operate a server that receives or stores this information.

## How Your Data Is Used

Data entered into ProductivityPet is used only to provide the app's features, such as managing tasks, tracking progress, and updating the virtual pet experience.

## Data Sharing

ProductivityPet does not sell or share users' personal information with third parties.

## Data Storage

ProductivityPet stores app data locally on the user's device. Removing the app or clearing its app data may permanently delete locally stored information.

## Children's Privacy

ProductivityPet is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as ProductivityPet gains new features. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
